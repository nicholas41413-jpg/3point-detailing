Questions

Frequently Asked Questions.

How long does a detail take?

Service time depends on the package and vehicle. The Premium Exterior Wash and Interior Detail are estimated at 1–2 hours, while the Full Detail is estimated at 2–3 hours. Actual time may vary depending on vehicle size and condition.

What vehicles do you detail?

We offer pricing for sedans, SUVs, and large SUVs and trucks.

How do I get a quote?

Use the quote request form on the website or contact 3Point Detailing directly.

What is included in the Full Detail?

The Full Detail combines all of the listed interior and exterior services, including the foam bath, wheel and tire cleaning, tire shine, ceramic paint sealant, interior vacuuming, crevice cleaning, interior wipe-down, UV protection, leather/fabric cleaning, and streak-free glass.

How long does the ceramic paint protection last?

The ceramic paint sealant provides up to 6 months of protection to the vehicle's paint. Actual durability can vary depending on conditions and maintenance.

Is there a mobile service fee?

Yes. A $10 mobile service fee applies when 3Point Detailing travels to the customer's location.

Is there an additional charge for pet hair?

Yes. Significant pet hair removal has an additional $25 fee because it requires additional cleaning time and work.

Can I get just an interior or exterior detail?

Yes. Customers can choose the Interior Detail, Premium Exterior Wash, or Full Detail depending on what their vehicle needs.
